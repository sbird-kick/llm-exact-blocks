# ASDiv rows: license

Derived from **ASDiv** ("A Diverse Corpus for Evaluating and Developing English Math Word Problem Solvers", Miao, Liang
and Su, ACL 2020; https://github.com/chaochun/nlu-asdiv-dataset), released under **CC BY-NC 4.0** (Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/); the dataset page says:
"This dataset is released under the CC BY-NC 4.0 license". Non-commercial research and teaching only; keep this
attribution with any copy.

Changes made here: prompts ending at " The answer is", a parsed gold program, a digit-run record and a fold label.
These are the ASDiv rows that are `train` in the project's in-force split and not already in `data/wild/asdiv/`.
