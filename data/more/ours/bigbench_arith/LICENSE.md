# bigbench_arith rows: license

The rows are the **BIG-bench `arithmetic` task** questions ("What is 12 plus 7?", 1 to 5 digit addition, subtraction,
multiplication and division; BIG-bench: "Beyond the Imitation Game: Quantifying and extrapolating the capabilities of
language models", BIG-bench authors, TMLR 2023, https://github.com/google/BIG-bench), which is under the **Apache
License 2.0** (the repository page states "Apache-2.0 license"; https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Changes made here: prompts ending at " The answer is", the gold equation read off the sentence, a digit-run record and a
fold label. Only the rows that are `train` in the project's in-force split.
