# bbx rows: license

Questions of the same sentence form as the BIG-bench `arithmetic` task ("What is <a> plus <b>?"; the template is copied
from BIG-bench's example generator, Apache License 2.0, https://github.com/google/BIG-bench) with 6 to 20 digit operands
drawn by the project (MIT, like the repo). Only the rows that are `train` in the project's in-force split.
