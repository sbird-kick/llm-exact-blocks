# wildbig rows: license

These rows are the Ape210K and Math23K problems of `data/more/wild/` with their numerals redrawn to 6, 9 or 12 digits
(wording kept; written by the project's `wildbig` builder). They are derivatives of those problems and carry **their**
license status (Ape210K: not clearly stated; Math23K: not found), see `../../wild/ape210k/LICENSE.md` and
`../../wild/math23k/LICENSE.md`. Kept for the owner's private use (repo owner's decision, 2026-10-20); clear before
making them public.
